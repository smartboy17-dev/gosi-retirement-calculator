Privacy Policy – CIA Practice

Effective date: May 11, 2026

CIA Practice ("the App") is developed and maintained by AbdulMohsen. This privacy policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. All data (quiz answers, bookmarks, progress) is stored locally on your device using Android's shared preferences storage.

2. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or log in. No email address, name, or any other personal information is requested.

3. No Third-Party Analytics or Ads

The App does not contain any advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking tools. Your usage data stays entirely on your device.

4. Internet Access

The App does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any remote server.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults preparing for the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) examination. It is not directed at children under the age of 13.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
